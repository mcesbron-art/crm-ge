# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sales and commercial teams managing client relationships, opportunities, and project pipelines. Users work in a fast-paced environment, switching between client details, deal tracking, project timelines, and billing information. The system serves as the single source of truth for revenue-generating activities and team coordination.

## Product Purpose

A customer relationship management platform for Groupe Écho that centralizes client data, sales pipelines, project management, and billing in one unified interface. Success means teams close deals faster, collaborate seamlessly across roles, and have visibility into all client-related activities in real time.

## Brand Personality

Professional, Trustworthy, Sophisticated. The design communicates expertise and confidence without unnecessary ornamentation. Users should feel they're working with a serious, business-grade tool that respects their time and expertise.

## Anti-references

- Flat, generic SaaS dashboards with no visual distinction
- Cluttered interfaces with poor information hierarchy
- Overly playful or casual aesthetics that undermine credibility
- Dashboard clichés (big number cards, gradient accents, rounded everything)

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — every visual element serves a purpose; no ornamentation
2. **Efficient scanning** — users should find any piece of information in seconds
3. **Professional restraint** — sophisticated typography and spacing, not trendy effects
4. **Role-aware workflows** — different views and access for sales, PM, admin, and finance roles
5. **Speed first** — keyboard navigation, fast interaction, minimal loading states

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance required. Specific focus on:
- Contrast ratios ≥4.5:1 for body text and interactive elements
- Keyboard navigation throughout (Tab, Enter, Escape patterns)
- Screen reader support for navigation, tables, and form fields
- Reduced motion support for animations
